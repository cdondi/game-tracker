---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user one question at a time about a feature or app idea until the requirements are specific. Use before writing a PRD.
---
Interview me about the idea I describe. Ask ONE question at a time, and wait for my answer.
Cover: users, core features, data fields, edge cases, what is out of scope, and how we will know it works.
Challenge vague answers. Propose a sensible default when I am unsure.
When the requirements feel complete, summarize them in 10 bullets and ask me to confirm.
